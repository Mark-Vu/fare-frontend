# Fare reference board

Reviewed: 2026-10-03

## Current product references

- GuideGeek: https://guidegeek.com/
  - Strongest trait: one chat carries inspiration, live price comparison, and booking intent.
  - Borrowed principle: show flights, stays, and activities as one continuous conversation.
- Mindtrip: https://mindtrip.ai/
  - Strongest trait: preferences are remembered and recommendations explain their tradeoffs.
  - Borrowed principle: connect each recommendation to a named traveler constraint.
- Layla: https://layla.ai/
  - Strongest trait: destination imagery is attached to concrete trips and people.
  - Borrowed principle: photos should prove a trip outcome, not decorate generic feature copy.

## Asset cast

- Hero anchor: `/public/images/hero-japan-cove.png`
- Destination range: Tokyo street, Lisbon rooftop, Costa Rica lodge
- Process artifact: scroll-linked WhatsApp planning thread
- Product proof: constraint ledger, live plan surface, and group decision ledger
- Texture object: warm WhatsApp paper pattern inside the iPhone shell
- Micro assets: Tabler icons at a consistent 2px stroke weight

## Direction contract

- Thesis: the group should never need to leave its natural conversation to make a trip decision.
- First viewport: one photographic destination, one precise product promise, two quiet actions.
- System: warm forest and sun palette, compact editorial spacing, asymmetric image-led sections.
- Risk: a device mockup can become decorative. The phone must always carry the actual workflow.

## Rejected patterns

- Static floating chat cards
- Uniform three-up feature rows
- Fabricated testimonials or metrics
- Large empty gaps between stacked sections
- Repeated pill labels and decorative AI sparkle motifs
